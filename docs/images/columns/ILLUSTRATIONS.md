# Development column illustrations

These three original conceptual illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool for the VvfM development columns. They are not application screenshots, hardware diagrams, measurements, or evidence of particular performance results.

- `development-01-eyecatch.png`: profiling CPU/GPU work and selectively updating vertices, represented by two processing tiles, a silver laptop, and a wireframe dancer with a small teal-lit vertex cluster
- `development-02-eyecatch.png`: observation and controlled debugging of gradual playback slowdown, represented by successive dancer poses, timing beads, and a magnifying lens; does not depict a confirmed SECounter mechanism

## Prompt direction

All three used a wide 16:9 composition, premium minimalist editorial 3D illustration, an ivory seamless studio surface, silver laptop, delicate wireframe dancing mannequin, subtle paper texture and soft shadows. No text, labels, numbers, charts, logos, watermark, branded characters or fake application interface were requested.

1. CPU/GPU optimization: selective glowing vertices and orderly light streams linking processing tiles to a dancer; restrained cyan and teal accents
2. Playback investigation: successive translucent dancing poses and a clear magnifying lens focused on timing beads; restrained blue and periwinkle accents; observation rather than a known causal explanation
3. Creator effects: translucent abstract instruction tiles transformed into theatrical violet, cyan and pale pink light around a wireframe dancer; a visual metaphor for translation and creative expression
